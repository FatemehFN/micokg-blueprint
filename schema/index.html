    MiCoKnowGraph schema¶

    Draft schema for a microbiome knowledge graph (BioHackathon Europe 2026, Project 16). Three layers: entities identified by ontology terms, SOSA observations on samples, and associations stored as nodes that carry context and evidence. Identifier mappings are kept outside the schema, in mappings/.

    URI: https://w3id.org/micokg

    Name: micokg
