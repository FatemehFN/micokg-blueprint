# Introduction

Microbiome research produces data across genomic, imaging, metabolic, clinical and environmental domains. Knowledge graphs (KGs) have been built for bacteria–host interactions and microbiome–disease associations, but they remain fragmented and don't interoperate. At BioHackathon Europe 2026 the ELIXIR Microbiome Community set out to design a unified, interoperable KG schema, together with the VERDANT project (Project 11).
